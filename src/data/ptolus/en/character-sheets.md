This guide ends with printable character sheets: three pages for 5th Edition and two pages for the Cypher System. They are reproduced here as images from the original PDF; open an image to view it at full size.

![Fifth Edition character sheet, page 1](/ptolus-sheet-5e-1.webp)

The first three pages are a single Fifth Edition sheet split across three pages: the first page carries abilities, skills, and attacks, the second page holds appearance, backstory, and contacts, and the third page covers equipment, coin, and additional traits.
