### Starting Move

#### ☑ <a id="rage!"></a>Rage\!

When you enter a rage, mark Angry. When you calm down, you may mark a different Condition to clear Angry. While Angry, you get the following penalties and benefits: When you deal max dmg, increase the dmg by STR Ignore penalties from Conditions when you [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\))\+STR or [Defy Danger](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#defy-danger-\(+variable\))\+STR Whenever you would mark a Condition (including from calming down), you may instead suffer 1d4 dmg `#piercing` You can't benefit from wearing armor (you can still wear a shield, though) If you don't act on your Rage at least once per scene you're in (usually attacking someone or something), mark an additional Condition at the end of that scene

### Berserker

*You’re the hardest of them, with a touch of the uncanny*

#### ☑ Trance-Like Fury

Gain the [Rage\!](#rage!) Barbarian Starting Move. If you already have [Rage\!](#rage!), then you get the following additional benefits and penalties: Add your STR to your armor When you calm down, mark an additional Condition When you [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)), you and your foe have adv to their dmg rolls

#### ☐ Red Nails

Once per session, when you become Bloodied, clear a Condition. While Bloodied, increase all dmg you deal by 1\. When you deal dmg to a Bloodied foe, it's `#piercing`.

#### ☐ Spine-Chilling Countenance

When you [Pull Strings](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#pull-strings-\(+cha\)) by physically overpowering or manhandling them, roll+STR (instead of \+CHA) and, if they go back on their word later, gain adv the next Move you roll against them.

#### ☐ The Beast Within

When you unleash the beast inside while Angry, you shapeshift into a hybrid form between yours and one of following animals: *Bear*. You Resist mundane weapons and fire *Boar*. You Resist fear, terror, and intimidation, even from supernatural sources *Wolf*. Increase your [dmg dice size](/ko/awe/dw2/character-creation#damage-dice-size) one step To put the beast back under requires a great effort; mark a Condition.

#### When It Earns Me

When you [Face Death](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death) while Angry, you may also choose the option below. *Spit in Death’s Face*. Recover all HP and you don’t become Helpless. Don't lock a Condition either. When you do, lose access to this Move until you gain it as an advancement again.

### Outlander

*You can’t take the homeland out of the…* 

#### ☑ Where I Come From…

When you tell someone a new story from your homeland (or about its practices, stories, traditions, etc.), mark and answer a question below and clear a Condition. When all five are marked, gain 1 XP and clear all questions. *What drawback or failure of your homeland still bothers you? What heroes or monsters feature in your homeland's stories? What food, art, or event is central to your homeland's lifestyle? What unique wonder or danger is native to your homeland? Who from your homeland do you look up to—or despise?*

#### ☐ Pack Reader

When you Sense the Motive of a group of people, you may ask an extra question below no matter the roll. *What recent event still troubles or excites them? Who have they outcast recently, and where can I find them? Who is hiding a secret from the others? Who's really in charge here?*

#### ☐ Sacrificial Rite

When you ritually sacrifice something significant to your homeland’s wardens (be it ancestors, deities, spirits, totem, etc.), roll+WIS. \
* **10+:** choose all three. \
* **7–9:** only two. Ask the GM a question about an enemy; they’ll answer honestly Everyone who participated in the ritual clears a Condition Gain a blessing proportional to the offering you made. ask for what you want, the GM will say what you get \
* **6-:** choose one, but the wardens will not accept your sacrifices again until you’ve fulfilled a demand; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ Tread the Jeweled Thrones

When you [Sense the Motive](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sense-motive-\(+wis\)) of someone more powerful than you (politically, physically, economically, etc.), you may always also ask them “*How can I best ruin or humiliate you?*”. When you accomplish it, mark 1 XP and clear a Condition.

#### ☐ Unerring Intuition

When you arrive at a place you haven’t visited before, your instincts tell you more than your senses ever could; roll+WIS. \
* **10+:** the GM will tell you what’s terribly wrong here; ask a follow-up question and gain adv to the first Move you roll when acting on the answer. \
* **7–9:** as a 10+ but the answer disturbs or unsettles you; mark a Condition. \
* **6-:** you get a cryptic hint…And nothing more; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

### Hedonist

*You know what is best in life, and how to  take it*

#### Herculean Appetite — ☐ Unsatified

You have a grand and terrible need that pushes you to greater heights and deeper depths. Say what it is now — Appetite: \_\_\_ Examples include: *fame and glory, mortal pleasures, power over others, riches and property, the pride of my ancestors, the thrill of chaos, utter destruction.* You have the additional Condition, Unsatisfied. It can be marked, locked, and cleared the same as any Condition, and [Reach Your Limit](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#reach-your-limit) only triggers when it has also been marked. While Unsatisfied is marked, you have dis to all Move rolls that don’t pursue satisfaction of your appetite. Whenever you look to satisfy your appetite, ask the GM how to pursue it here and now; they will tell you, usually with a risk or cost. When you satiate it, clear Unsatisfied. If locked, it only clears if you *gratuitously overindulge* it, usually with extreme cost and/or risk.

#### ☐ Basking in Victory

When you satiate an appetite to the extreme (destroy something precious, gain enormous fame, mountains of riches, etc.), in addition to clearing Unsatisfied, choose two: Clear an additional Condition Gain 1 Depth with a PC that helped you satisfy it Mark 1 XP Rewrite one of your appetites

#### ☐ Forthright Speech

When you smash through social etiquette to call something what it really is, roll+CHA. \
* **10+:** name someone here who agrees and is willing to help, and clear a Condition. \
* **7–9:** you find an ally or clear a Condition (your call). \
* **6-:** you’ll regret your honesty; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ Insatiable — Appetite\_\_\_

You have a second appetite. *What is it? Did you always have it, or develop it over time?* While you're Unsatisfied, you only need to pursue one of your appetites to not suffer its penalty. When you look to satisfy your appetite, tell the GM which one before they tell you how.

#### ☐ Strong as a Bull

When you strain yourself to break free of a restraint or compulsion (physical or mental), you can mark a Condition to automatically succeed.
