### Evoker

*There’s a simple beauty in utter destruction*

#### ☑ Evocation Magic

Gain the [Weave the Arcane](#weave-the-arcane) Wizard Starting Move, though you only have access to \
Evocation (substance: elemental energies; verbs: lesser sculpt; minor channel). If you already have this Move, instead add the verbs detonate and magnify to the Evocation School's repertoire, and when you [Make Camp](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp) you can always mark Evocation in addition to your normal 3 Prepared Schools. *Mental Reserves* — You can mark a Condition to instantly Prepare a School of Magic, up to your normal limit.

#### ☐ Contingency

Mark 1-3 Conditions then choose a spell or a Move that you have access to that you could reasonably prepare in advance. Also, define a specific circumstance. When the circumstance occurs, roll+Conditions marked; use the result to resolve the spell or Move.

#### ☐ Elemental Cloak

Mark 1-3 Conditions to evoke a cloak made of one of the following elements. Replace X with the amount of Conditions marked: *cascading flames* — Up to X opponents who inflict dmg to you suffer Xd6 dmg *icy garments* — Up to X opponents who inflict dmg to you have dis to their dmg rolls *rock protrusions* — Add \+X to your armor for X attacks *wild gale* — You can mobilize unimpeded up to `#near` X times

#### ☐ Instant Fireball\!

Mark 1-3 Conditions, then a `#near` target and everyone `#close` to them suffers Xd6 dmg `#piercing`, where X is the amount of Conditions marked.

#### ☐ Power Weapon

When you shape an elemental energy in the form of a weapon, mark 1-3 Conditions then roll+Conditions marked. \
* **10+:** choose two of the following tags. \
* **7–9:** only one: `#close` `#piercing` By default the weapon inflicts 1d6 dmg, is `#hand`, and has ◯◯◯ uses. When you Trade Blows with this weapon, roll+INT (instead of \+STR or \+DEX) and mark one use. \
* **6-:** the element breaks free; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

### Illusionist

*How do you define ‘real’?*

#### ☑ Illusion Magic

Gain the [Weave the Arcane](#weave-the-arcane) Wizard Starting Move, though you only have access to \
Illusion (substance: sights and/or sounds; verbs: cloak; disguise) School. If you already have this Move, instead add the verbs make permanent and substantiate to the Illusion School's repertoire, and when you [Make Camp](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp) you can always mark Illusion in addition to your normal 3 Prepared Schools. *Trickster* — Once per scene, when someone believes a lie because of one of your illusions, clear a Condition.

#### ☐ Alter Perception

When you touch someone or something, you can alter one of their perceptible aspects without [Casting a Spell](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\)); mark a Condition if they are unwilling. For example, you can change someone's perceived ancestry, outfit, or accent, or you can make an item seem magical, brand new, or made of a different substance. This effect lasts until you dismiss it or until the end of the Scene.

#### ☐ Fool Me Once…

When you trick someone with an illusion, the next time you [Pull Strings](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#pull-strings-\(+cha\)) on them you have adv and may roll+INT (instead of \+CHA), even if they learn it was an illusion. You can share these benefits with other PCs that were part of the illusion.

#### ☐ Mirror Image

When you would suffer dmg, you may mark a Condition to reveal that you were an illusion; prevent all dmg and say where `#near` you actually are.

#### ☐ The Mirror Stares Back

The first time each NPC falls for one of your illusions, you may ask them one question as if you [Sensed Motive](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sense-motive-\(+wis\)). When you [Cast a Spell](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\)) to make an illusory copy of a person, you can also mark a Condition to overcharge the spell. If you do, the illusory person perfectly imitates their original, lasts indefinitely, and starts loyal but slowly gains autonomy over time. You cannot dismiss an overcharged illusory person.

### Necromancer

*The greatest taboo among magic practitioners—for a reason*

#### ☑ Necromantic Magic

Gain the [Weave the Arcane](#weave-the-arcane) Wizard Starting Move, though you only have access to \
Necromancy (substance: unlife; verbs: briefly bestow; radiate). If you already have this Move, instead add the verbs corrupt and drain to the Necromancy School's repertoire, and when you [Make Camp](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp) you can always mark Necromancy in addition to your normal 3 Prepared Schools. *Rise and Serve* — When you kill a living creature with a spell, you can create a ghoul, skeleton, or zombie from their body (2 HP, inflicts 1 dmg).Undead you create are loyal to you and follow your commands, though only `#intelligent` ones (which these are not) can understand complex tasks.

#### ☐ A Ghost at the Feast

When you kill a living creature with a spell, instead of creating something from their corpse, you can create a ghost, shade, or shadow from their soul (1 HP, inflicts 2 dmg `#piercing` | `#invisible` `#immaterial`). If you mark a Condition, you can create two undead—one from body and one from soul.

#### ☐ Lifeforce Is a Resource

When you [Cast a Spell](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\)), \
* **7–9:** you can instead choose this: *it drains your lifeforce*; take 1 dmg `#piercing` When you attune yourself to the ethereal shackles that bind your undead to your will, you may mark a Condition, then redestribute HP between yourself and any of your `#near` undead as you see fit.

#### ☐ Sanctum to Unlife

When you set your mind to imbue a location with unholy power, treat it as [Perform a Ritual](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#perform-a-ritual), but you can sacrifice living people or your own *powerful* undead 1-for-1 to negate each requirement—except for the last one*.* After you succeed, for each requirement the GM originally gave you, choose one benefit below: Any creature that dies here automatically rises as an `#undead` that serves you `#holy` power is weak here, dealing half dmg (rounded down) and lessening other effects It acts as a source of power for even darker rituals It will attract willing devotees and followers The change will last indefinitely (unless actively cleansed at its heart)

#### ☐ Shed this Mortal Coil

You've learned the secrets of lichdom. Gain a [*Phylactery*](/ko/awe/dw2/example-magic-items#phylactery) (see *Magic Items*).
