## <a id="major-npcs"></a>Major NPCs

A Non-Player Character (NPC) is any character that the GM portrays: friends, foes, people, animals, monsters, spirits, or strangers. Each session typically involves various NPCs across different Scenes, which act as the most active part of the adventurer's surroundings.

All NPCs have the following traits:

* A **Name**, maybe of them individually, maybe just of the type of NPC they are collectively, plus what pronouns they use (if applicable)  
* A **Description** that summarizes who they are  
* A **Drive** that they primarily desire and pursue, be it an instinct, value, emotion, or vow  
* A short list of **Resources** that they typically rely on to pursue and achieve their Drive

Additionally, an NPC may have all or any of these traits:

* If they have notable defenses or preparations against something, they might **Resist** some things, meaning they are unaffected by them by default (though this may be overcome narratively)  
* If they know how to fight, then they’ll have **HP**, **dmg**, and **Armor**  
* If they're a relevant NPC in the fiction, usually a major antagonist or obstacle, they'll have **Conditions** that cause **Escalations** when marked. Each Condition can be emotional or situational, and don't need to be the same as the PCs’ Conditions. When an NPC suffers significant dmg or setback (your call as the GM), you can mark a Condition and have them **Escalate**, pulling the spotlight back to them and [**Making the Situation Worse**](/ko/awe/dw2/gm-moves#make-the-situation-worse) in the process.

### Fighting NPCs

When an NPC inflicts dmg, usually from a PC [**Trading Blows**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)) with them, the GM rolls the dmg openly. When an NPC suffers dmg, reduce their HP by that same amount (just as PCs do).

An NPC is defeated when they either reach 0 HP or mark all of their Conditions (after or as part of their final Escalation). NPCs who aren't tough in a fight generally have no HP or dmg, meaning they are defeated after taking a single hit, or even without rolling.

When time passes, such as when the PCs [**Make Camp**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp), then NPCs can restore some or all of their lost HP and clear their Conditions. You can also restore HP or Conditions in the moment if the fiction fits, such as incredible regeneration abilities, but this should be rare and exceptional.

### Creating an NPC Cheat Sheet

1. *Who are they and what do they look like?* Write as much or as little of this down as their **Description**  
2. *What core goal or desire do they protect or advance?* Write that down as their **Drive**  
3. *What do they use to pursue their Drive?* These could be approaches, tools, methods, weapons, or other things; write these down as their **Resources**

   *For a dragon this could be* teeth, claws, and fire*. A merchant might use* an always-friendly face, an eye for appraisal, *and* connections with everyone

4. *What are they particularly defended against or prepared for?* Write this down as what they **Resist**

   *A knight with a tower shield might* **Resist:** arrows*, while a master ranger might* **Resist:** people sneaking up on them in the wilderness

5. *How relevant do you expect them to be to the fiction?* Give them an appropriate number of Conditions and Escalations following these guidelines:

* *Barely a character at all* — 0 Conditions & Escalations  
* *Minor character with a single trait* — 1 Condition & Escalation  
* *Relevant character who spends time in the spotlight* — 2-3 Conditions & Escalations  
* *Critically relevant character, candidate to be a major antagonist to the PCs* — 4-5 Conditions & Escalations

6. *Do they know how to fight?* If “Yes”, continue on. If not, you're done\!

7. *How do they usually fight?* This determines their **starting dmg** and **HP**.

* *In large groups* — d4 dmg, 3 HP  
* *In small groups, about 2–5* — d6 dmg, 6 HP  
* *All by its lonesome* — d8 dmg, 12 HP

8. *How big are they?*

* *Smaller than a house cat* — `#hand`, reduce [dmg die size](/ko/awe/dw2/character-creation#damage-dice-size) by one step  
* *Between halfling and human sized* — `#close` `#hand`  
* *As big as a cart* — `#close` `#hand` `#near`, \+4 HP, increase [dmg die size](/ko/awe/dw2/character-creation#damage-dice-size) by one step  
* *Much larger than a cart* — `#close` `#far` `#hand` `#near`, \+8 HP, increase [dmg die size](/ko/awe/dw2/character-creation#damage-dice-size) by two steps  
* *Can devour ships or houses whole* — `#close` `#far` `#near`, \+12 HP, increase [dmg die size](/ko/awe/dw2/character-creation#damage-dice-size) by three steps

9. *What is their most important defense?*

* *Cloth or flesh* — Armor 0  
* *Leathers or thick hide* — Armor 1  
* *Mail or scales* — Armor 2  
* *Plate or bone* — Armor 3  
* *Permanent magical protection* — Armor 4 and/or list what it Resists

10. *Which of these describes them?* Choose all that apply:

* *Abhors violence or especially frail* — reduce damage die size by one step  
* *Actively defends themselves with a shield or similar* — \+1 Armor  
* *Usually attacks at range* — `#near` `#far` or both (your call)  
* *Especially deadly* — increase damage die size by one step  
* *Prone to extreme emotion or outbursts* — \+1 Condition & Escalation

### Example NPCs 

#### Adult Red Dragon (pro/nouns)

*A flying titanic lizard who breathes fire & is seemingly impervious*  
**Drive** destroy all who threaten their lair  
**Resources** enormous teeth and claws, incinerating fire, tail spikes  
**Resists** mundane blows, engage in conversation with anyone who’s “boring”  
**dmg** 1d12+1d8 `#far` (bite, claws, and `#fire` dragon breath) **HP** 24/\_\_\_ **Armor** 2 vs extraordinary blows (layers of scales)  
*Conditions* & **Escalations**

- ☐ *Annoyed* — **Tear Something to Pieces**  
- ☐ *Proud* — **Take to the Skies**  
- ☐ *Nervous* — **Conjure Allies of Living Flame**  
- ☐ *Furious* — **Ignite Everything in the Scene**  
- ☐ *Afraid* — **Escape to Enact Future Vengeance**

#### Infamous Thief (pro/nouns)

*Friend or foe? Hard to know with a face-covering mask*  
**Drive** take what’s theirs in their mind  
**Resources** acrobatics, custom gadgets, poisons, stealth, and tricks  
**Resists** being seen when in shadows, faster reflexes, subterfuge   
**dmg** 1d8 `#close` `#hand` **HP** 12/\_\_\_ **Armor** 1 (well-oiled leather armor)  
*Conditions* & **Escalations** 

- ☐ *Endangered* — **Unleash Dangerous Poison**  
- ☐ *Cornered* — **Escape with Shadows and Smoke**

#### Mutated Grizzly Bear (pro/nouns)

*Probably the single most dangerous animal when provoked*  
**Drive** eat everyone it encounters OR protecting her children  
**Resources** incredible strength, sharp claws and teeth, thick fur  
**Resists** pushes, restraints, and barriers  
**dmg** 1d12 **HP** 16/\_\_\_ **Armor** 1 (thick fur)  
*Conditions* & **Escalations**

- ☐ *Enraged* — **Maul a Threat**  
- ☐ *Starving* — **Flee with Prey**

#### Goblin Horde

*“When you see one there are nine more just out of sight”*  
**Drive** steal whatever they can  
**Resources** cohesive teamwork, giant pet bugs, hidden tunnels, rapid scavenging, worn weapons   
**Resists** individual attacks, staying trapped for long  
**dmg** 1d8 `#close` `#hand` `#near` **HP** 11 **Armor** 4 (salvaged leathers and metal pieces) vs attacks to the whole horde  
*Conditions* & **Escalations**

- ☐ *Annoyed* — **Jump onto Someone’s Back and Start Biting**  
- ☐ *Bold* — **Reinforce with Insect Mounts**  
- ☐ *Terrified* — **Retreat Underground**
