### Starting Move

#### ☑ <a id="perform-your-art"></a>Perform Your Art

You express yourself and affect others with a particular artistic skill. *Is it cooking, music, poetry, or something else?* When you perform your art for an audience, roll+CHA \
* **10+:** choose three from below. \
* **7–9:** only two. \
* **6-:** you feel the performance was awful; choose one below, but mark a Condition. An option chosen more than once must apply to a new audience member. *Affect* — An audience member clears a marked Condition *Connect —* A PC audience member marks 1 Depth with you *Craft* — Regain one use of artistic samples (see below) *Dazzle* — An NPC audience member gives you a gift or tries to meet you privately (their call)

### Dabbler

*Jack of all trades, master of none*

#### ☑ Bardic Lore

You’ve studied a variety of arcane subjects, including: ☐ *A Bestiary of Creatures Most Unusual* ☐ *Gods and Their Servants* ☐ *Grand Histories of the Known World* ☐ *Legends of Heroes Past* ☐ *Spells and Magicks* ☐ *The Dead and Undead* ☐ *The Planar Spheres*. Mark one as active when you choose this Move. You can ask the GM questions about your active subject a number of times per session equal to your INT (min 1); the GM will answer truthfully without the need to Spout Lore. When you [Make Camp](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp), you can spend some time studying and/or remembering your training to change your active subject.

#### ☐ A Kind of Magic

You can [Cast a Spell](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\))\+INT by combining a verb and a substance from the Wizard’s ☐ \
Enchantment (substance: sapient beings; verbs: modify emotions; temporarily charm) or ☐ \
Illusion (substance: sight and/or sounds; verbs: cloak; disguise) Schools. To Cast a Spell this way you need to do a performance, and your unique 7-9 option is: *Your magic is drained*; forget one of the Schools of Magic you have access to until you next [Make Camp](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp) If you could already use these Schools, work with the GM to create a new verb for each redundant School.

#### ☐ A Knave, a Rascal 

You get access to the following Roguish Skills: \
✦ *climb walls* \
✦ *detect noises* \
✦ *pick pockets* \
✦ *understand languages*. When you [Defy Danger](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#defy-danger-\(+variable\)) or [Sneak Past](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sneak-past-\(+dex\)) using one of your Roguish Skills, you may roll\+INT (instead of another Stat). If you already had any of these Roguish Skills, work with the GM to create a new one for each redundant Skill.

#### ☐ Artisan's Apprentice

Gain the [Perform Your Art](#perform-your-art) Starting Move from the Bard Class. If you already have [Perform Your Art](#perform-your-art), you gain a second artistic skill. When you [Perform Your Art](#perform-your-art) by showcasing both artistic skills together, make an additional choice (no matter the roll).

#### ☐ Virtuoso’s Mind

You can have up to two Bardic Lore subjects active at a time. When you ask the GM a question about an active subject, instead of them answering you may choose to [Spout Lore](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#spout-lore-\(+questions\)) with adv. \
* **12+:** you may answer (instead of the GM).

### Diplomat

*Mastery of people is an art in and unto itself*

#### ☑ The Oldest Game

When you accomplish a significant task for a relevant NPC (GM's call), now you hold a Debt against them. While you hold a Debt over someone: Treat any 6- when you Pull Strings on them as a 7-9 You can always ask them “*What do you* really *need right now?*” when you [Sense Motive](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sense-motive-\(+wis\)), regardless of the roll They'll give you insignificant favors (in their eyes), such as: small funding; introduction; borrowing of possessions for a limited time; etc. When you cash in your Debt, choose one: Name something they have; they’ll give it to you Point them at an enemy or obstacle; they’ll act against it Tell them you’re in trouble; they’ll protect you

#### ☐ Cards on the Table

When you reveal your true intentions to a foe or stranger, you may ask them a number of questions from the list below, up to your CHA (min 1). They will answer honestly, but ask a question back from the list in return each time. At the end of the scene, if you lied in your own answers, lock a Condition. *How might I convince you to \_\_\_? What do you most want me to do? What leverage or advantage do you have? What obligation or vulnerability is limiting you? Who or what do you serve?*

#### ☐ Countercharm

When you try to impede a mind-influencing effect, roll\+CHA. \
* **10+:** it doesn’t affect you or your allies. \
* **7–9:** you *or* your allies are unaffected (your call). \
* **6-:** it’s beyond your ken; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move. Once per scene, when you or an ally would mark a Condition due to external influence, you (or they) don’t.

"External influence" could be emotional magic, mind control, an aura of fearful dread, or any number of things. Generally, it happens when the GM tells you to mark a specific Condition due the fiction, instead of when a Move you made tells you to mark one.

#### ☐ Friends in High Places

When you seek the help of a relevant NPC, roll+CHA. If you owe them a favor (your call) or hold a Debt against them, roll with adv. \
* **10+:** they’re free and able to help. \
* **7–9:** they’re in trouble and can’t help until that’s dealt with. \
* **6-:** you find only treachery or tragedy; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ Parley\!

When you call for a truce or ceasefire, even mid-battle, roll+CHA. If you know what all parties want (or you hold a Debt against them), roll with adv. \
* **10+:** as a 7-9 but you can also ask them one question as if you [Sensed Motive](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sense-motive-\(+wis\)). \
* **7–9:** hostilities pause and you get a chance to negotiate. \
* **6-:** they misread or exploit your intent; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

### Swashbuckler

*There’s beauty in all things, even fighting\!*

#### ☑ Style Is Everything — Panache ☐ ☐ ☐

Once per Scene, when you do something in an unnecessarily dramatic manner, you may mark a Condition to reset Panache to equal your CHA. While you have Panache, when you [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)) using a `#precise` weapon, you can roll+CHA (instead of \+DEX), and you may spend 1 Panache to gain adv on the roll.

#### ☐ Fly Into the Face of Danger

When you [Defy Danger](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#defy-danger-\(+variable\)) by attempting something you’ve never tried before, after the roll you may spend 1 Panache to treat a 6- as if it were 7-9, or a 7-9 as if it were a 10+.

#### ☐ *I* Am Not Left-Handed\!

When you [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)), after you roll you can spend 1 Panache to do one of the following: *Disarm* — \
* **10+:** you make your opponent drop something they’re holding (instead of the other choices) *Feint* — `#pierce` your opponent's armor, ignoring it for this blow *Parry* — Reduce dmg received by your CHA, to a minimum of 0

#### ☐ Very Intricate Backstory

You can have two Struggles active at the same time. Now or the next time you [Make Camp](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp), clear a locked Condition (if possible) or [Level Up](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#level-up) and [Reveal Your Struggle](/ko/awe/dw2/campaign-moves#reveal-your-struggle).
