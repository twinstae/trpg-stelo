### Starting Move

#### ☑ <a id="tricks-of-the-trade"></a>Tricks of the Trade

When you [Defy Danger](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#defy-danger-\(+variable\)) or [Sneak Past](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sneak-past-\(+dex\)) by applying one of your Roguish Skills, you cheat; roll with 1d8+1d6 (instead of 2d6). You start with the following Roguish Skills:

* *detect noises*
* *find/remove traps*
* *hide in shadows*
* *open locks*
* *pick pockets* …plus any other Skills provided by your Paths.

### Assassin

*Silent as the grave, deadly as nightshade*

#### ☑ Dirty Deeds

Add *employ/cure poison* and *identify weakness* to your Roguish Skills. If you don't have the [Tricks of the Trade](#tricks-of-the-trade) Rogue Starting Move, you gain it, but only with these two Skills. If you already had any of these Roguish Skills, work with the GM to create a new one for each redundant Skill. *Sneak Attack* — When you [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\))\+DEX, if your opponent is distracted or flanked (and you're wearing light or no armor),

* **10+:** you can choose to deal max dmg for free.
* **7–9:** you suffer min dmg.

#### ☐ Always a Way Out

When you’re in too deep and look for a way out, roll+DEX.

* **10+:** you and your allies are gone.
* **7–9:** you need to leave something behind; the GM will say what.
* **6-:** it was a trick or a trap; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ Backstab

When you ambush a surprised or defenseless opponent with a `#precise` weapon, you may roll dmg as normal, or may instead roll+DEX.

* **10+:** choose two from the following.
* **7–9:** only one: Reduce their armor dmg reduction by 1 until it's repaired Disable a weapon or other form of attack until they recover it Inflict your dmg\+1d6
* **6-:** they were waiting for you; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ Poisoncraft

You get adv to [Defy Danger](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#defy-danger-\(+variable\)) against poison, and you get a poisoner’s kit ◯◯◯ uses. Mark to produce a poison on the spot; state its effect, then choose two from the following:

* it can be delivered by touch (otherwise it’s ingestion)
* the GM doesn't add an "and" or "but" to the effect (otherwise they do)
* it’s undetectable
* you clear a poisoner’s kit use

#### ☐ Stalker in Shadows

Add *hide in shadows* to your Roguish Skills. If you already have it, instead work with the GM to create a new Roguish Skill. When you [Sneak Past](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sneak-past-\(+dex\)) with a Roguish Skill, roll with 1d10+1d6 (instead of 1d8+1d6). When you look for a hiding space, the GM will tell you where the best spot is, but it's up to you to get there.

### Burglar

*Skill and guile—and a dash of good luck, of course* 

#### ☑ Expert Treasure-hunter

Add *climb walls* and *fit into tight spaces* to your Roguish Skills. If you don't have the [Tricks of the Trade](#tricks-of-the-trade) Rogue Starting Move, you gain it, but only with these two Skills. If you already had any of these Roguish Skills, work with the GM to create a new one for each redundant Skill. *Nose for Treasure* — When you [Unearth Secrets](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#unearth-secrets-\(+int\)), no matter the outcome of the roll, you may also ask the GM, *Are there valuables here I don’t know about?* If yes, gain adv on the next roll to pursue them.

#### ☐ Better Lucky Than Good — Luck ☐ ☐ ☐

When you take this Move, gain Luck equal to your WIS (min 1). Once per scene, you can spend 1 Luck to reroll any die rolled by you or that directly affects you (such as the dmg roll of an enemy attacking you). When you [Enjoy Downtime](/ko/awe/dw2/campaign-moves#enjoy-downtime), you can spend 1 Treasure helping an NPC to regain Luck equal to your WIS (min 1\).

#### ☐ Breaking & Entering

When you set your mind to steal something, name the thing, then ask the GM a number of questions below equal to your WIS (min 1\) and they will answer truthfully. When you acquire it, you and everyone who helped each clear a Condition. *What’s its most powerful defence? What's the best way in or out? Who else wants it? What else am I missing regarding it?*

#### ☐ Quick on the Draw

When the GM or another Player is about to make a Move, with their permission (out of character) you can mark a Condition to interrupt it and act immediately instead.

Please: don’t be an asshole and hog the spotlight with this Move. Use it in good faith (as everything else in the game).

#### ☐ Vanishing Act

When you are sure no one is paying attention directly to you, you can disappear from a Scene entirely, not rejoining the narrative until the Scene ends. If you’re in trouble or a bind, ask the GM what you must do to disappear and they will tell you up to 4-WIS of the following requirements: When you reappear, it will come with trouble You first need to risk/trick/steal \_\_\_ You must leave something significant behind You must mark a Condition in the process

### Spy

*Obtaining and trading secrets is your trade*

#### ☑ The One for the Job — Mask: ☐ ☐ ☐

Add *forge documents* and *understand languages* to your Roguish Skills. If you don't have the [Tricks of the Trade](#tricks-of-the-trade) Rogue Starting Move, you gain it, but only with these two Skills. For each Skill that is redundant, work with the GM to create a new one. *Smooth Operator* — When you disguise yourself as someone else, gain Mask equal to your CHA (min 1). You can spend Mask, 1-for-1, to automatically overcome scrutiny. When you reach 0 Mask, roll+CHA.

* **10+:** they're none the wiser; gain 1 Mask.
* **7–9:** someone suspects you; the GM will say who.
* **6-:** your cover is blown; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ Hand Trick

When you leave a room or other location, you can name something small that you saw that no one else was using or paying particular attention to. You took it with you.

#### ☐ Maven Saboteur

When you reveal that you've used a disguise to previously sabotage or nullify an obstacle or mechanism, right as it becomes relevant, roll\+CHA.

* **10+:** it goes off with a bang, creating the perfect opportunity.
* **7–9:** the opportunity is fleeting—act now or it'll be too late.
* **6-:** a tiny overlooked detail becomes a huge problem; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ Underworld Connections

When you put the word out that you’re looking for something, roll+CHA. No matter the result, someone brings you a lead.

* **10+:** choose one complication below.
* **7–9+:** choose two: It's not quite right; the GM will say how *after* you choose this Someone who doesn’t know or like you has it You’ll owe someone a steep debt to get it. Until you repay them you get dis on this Move and can’t choose this option again
* **6-:** the lead might be a fake, a trap, or already be claimed by an enemy; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ You Know My Name

When you first meet someone who’s heard of you (your call), roll+CHA.

* **10+:** tell the GM two things they’ve heard about you.
* **7–9:** tell the GM one thing, and the GM tells you the other.
* **6-:** you won’t like what they’ve heard; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.
