### Starting Move

#### ☑ <a id="shapeshift"></a>Shapeshift

When you transform into a beast you’ve encountered before, describe your new shape, choose an adaptation below, and roll+WIS. If you encountered the beast before the game began, the GM may ask for details.

- *fast & agile* — 1d6 dmg `#precise` `#hand` `#close`
- *mobile & acclimated* — 1d4 dmg `#hand`, can ☐ fly, ☐ swim, or ☐ burrow
- *small or camouflaged* — 1 dmg `#hand`. You have adv to [**Sneak Past**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sneak-past-\(+dex\))
- *tough & strong* — Armor 2, 1d8 dmg `#hand` `#close`

* **10+:** it works and you gain the adaptation’s benefits.
* **7–9:** it works, but the GM will say what compels your instincts; pursue it now or mark a Condition to resist it.
* **6-:** it works, but you lose control over it for now; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

While shapeshifted you can’t communicate with anyone except other PCs. You can return to normal any time you’re in control, but must always do so when the scene ends.

### Animist

*The natural world is alive in ways few mortals now understand*

#### ☑ <a id="anima-mundi"></a>Anima Mundi

You have an especially close connection with the elemental spirits of the world—the roots, rocks, winds, waters, and more—and they see you as a guardian, caretaker, and ambassador. You can [**Cast a Spell**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\)) by entreating natural spirits for aid. When you do so, your unique 7–9 option is:

* The spirits demand a favor or sacrifice; the GM will say more after you choose this

Each time you Cast a Spell this way, answer the questions below. Your Casting Stat for the spell starts at 0 and increases by 1 for each “Yes”.

* *Are you wielding and wearing no metal whatsoever?*
* *Does this leverage a nearby natural feature or entity?*
* *Will this restore harmony or advance a cycle of nature?*

#### ☐ Back to Basics

When you reveal a new flaw or failure of civilization to one or more NPCs, clear a Condition, then roll+CHA.

* **10+:** choose two below.
* **7–9:** only one.
* **6-:** you draw an enemy’s attention; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

- They account for the issue and start working to fix it
- They attack or sabotage an enemy of nature
- They protect you or nature from an imminent threat

#### ☐ Commune with Nature

When you spend time connecting with the natural spirits, ask one question below about the region you’re in, or ask all of them but mark a Condition after hearing the answers. The GM will answer honestly.

* *Does anything here threaten the natural world?*
* *What danger or enemy here should we be aware of?*
* *What person or location here could be helpful to us?*

#### ☐ Death & Regrowth — Lifeforce ☐ ☐

When you finish off a significant enemy, or when you suffer max dmg, gain 1 Lifeforce (max 2). You can spend Lifeforce as if it was a use of medicine.

#### ☐ Eye of the Storm

When you [**Cast a Spell**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\)) by entreating natural spirits for aid, if all three questions are answered “Yes”, you can treat the mag as if it were 1 lower when comparing it to your Casting Stat.

### Stargazer

*As foretold above, so unfolds below*

#### ☑ <a id="eye-in-the-sky"></a>Eye in the Sky

You have a special connection to the sun(s), moon(s), and stars. You always know the time and date, and can never get lost while you can see the sky. While outside during the day, ignore dis from being Frightened and gain \+1 Armor. While outside during the night, ignore dis from being Insecure and deal \+1 dmg.

#### ☐ Augury

When you read the future written in the stars, you can focus your reading on a specific place or person; roll+WIS, then the GM reveals an important event that will happen soon.

* **10+:** you may ask a follow-up question.
* **7–9:** the vision is disturbing; mark a Condition.
* **6-:** the event involves an ally’s terrible fate (PC or NPC); the GM will say how, but then you can twist the fate to fall upon you instead. Whoever suffers the terrible fate has dis on all rolls during the event.

#### ☐ Doomsayer

When you proclaim an enemy’s imminent fate to them, roll+WIS.

* **10+:** choose two.
* **7–9:** only one.
* **6-:** you only see their success; mark 1 XP and gain dis against them until the end of the scene.

- *Pain* — Increase dmg they take by 1 until the end of the scene
- *Regret* — Gain adv the next time you or an ally [**Pulls Strings**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#pull-strings-\(+cha\)) against them
- *Vulnerability* — The GM will tell you a weakness they hide

#### ☐ Forecaster

After you successfully [**Make Camp**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp), choose what the weather is like from the list below. It remains like this until someone changes it, you use this Move again, or you depart from the region. You and all companions gain the weather’s benefit.

* *clear skies* — Clear a Condition when this is chosen
* *overcast* — Ignore dis from being Exhausted
* *rain, fog, or snow* — Gain adv to [**Sneak Past**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sneak-past-\(+dex\)) outside

#### ☐ Scion of Sunrise and Sunset

When outside during dawn or dusk, gain [**Eye in the Sky**](#eye-in-the-sky)'s daytime and nighttime benefits. When you focus on your connection to the celestial bodies, you may mark a Condition to treat this scene as if it takes place outdoors during dawn or dusk.

### Wildheart

*Follower of the oldest path, wild & weary as the beasts*

#### ☑ Primeval Whisperer

You can communicate freely with wild creatures. When you [**Sense the Motive**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sense-motive-\(+wis\)) of a wild creature, no matter the result you may ask them an extra question, even on a 6-. When you [**Pull the Strings**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#pull-strings-\(+cha\)) of a wild creature, if your approach aligns with their desires or instincts, roll with adv.

A "wild creature" can be an animal, plant, fungus, or other non-sapient living being. It isn't a "thing" like a rock or a river, but it could be a tree or an insect. If it's unclear whether something is a wild creature, the GM has the final say.

#### ☐ Call of the Wild

When you call out to the wilderness for aid, choose a suitable type of wild creature for your current environment and roll+CHA. On a **7+**, that wild creature arrives to help you for the scene as an allied NPC.

* **10+:** choose two.
* **7–9:** only one.
* **6-:** you instead draw nature’s ire or hunger; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

- A pack or group of them arrive instead of just one
- They accompany you for a period after this scene ends
- They cause no collateral damage or turmoil while here
- They intimidate or distract an enemy upon their arrival

#### ☐ Raised by Wolves — Creature: \_\_\_

Choose one type of wild creature (e.g. bears, eagles, wolves). By default, they treat you as a friend and your companions as allies by association. When you spend time with your chosen wild creature, choose one if alone or two if with another PC.

* *exchange gifts* — Regain a spent use of an item
* *listen to their stories* — Learn about a nearby danger or obstacle as if you rolled a 7–9 to [**Spout Lore**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#spout-lore-\(+questions\))
* *relax and recharge* — Clear a Condition

#### ☐ Rumour From the Earth

When you [**Unearth Secrets**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#unearth-secrets-\(+int\)) or [**Sense Motive**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sense-motive-\(+wis\)) by questioning nearby wild creatures, you may roll \+CHA.

The wild creatures don't need to be the subject of your Unearthing or Sensing to get this benefit. For example, you could Unearth Secrets of a corrupt politician by asking the rats in their cellar what they've overheard.

#### ☐ Together as One

When you mark a wild creature with blood, mud, or dirt, erase previous marks, then you can share in their senses as if they were yours (and vice versa), no matter what distance separates you.
