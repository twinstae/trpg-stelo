### Starting Move

#### ☑ <a id="animal-companion"></a>Animal Companion — Name (pro/nouns): \_\_\_

You work closely with a reasonably-sized mundane natural beast. Examples include: bear, fox, hawk, hound, wolf, etc. Give them a name, describe their appearance, and consider how you bonded.

Then choose three **Qualities** they have:  
☐ aquatic ☐ burrowing ☐ cute ☐ fast ☐ flying ☐ intelligent ☐ intimidating ☐ large ☐ night vision ☐ stealthy ☐ strong ☐ tracker

And one **Drawback**:  
☐ clumsy ☐ cowardly ☐ fragile ☐ hungry ☐ loud ☐ stubborn

When **your animal companion assists you**, say how, then choose one for each relevant Quality (GM's call):

* If fighting, they increase your armor by 1 and your dmg dealt by 1d4
* Their Drawback doesn't introduce a complication
* They create an opportunity or overcome a small obstacle
* They grant you adv on an associated Move roll

Usually, you control and portray your animal companion along with your own PC. If they are separated from you or affected by their Drawback, the GM might control them for a time.

Your animal companion doesn't have HP. **When they get hurt**, you mark a Condition or say how they leave the scene.

### Beastmaster

*Two bodies, one soul*

#### ☑ No Mere Creature

Gain the [**Animal Companion**](#animal-companion) Ranger Starting Move if you don't already have it.

If you already have it, instead choose two additional Qualities for your Companion.

Either way, choose and mark one Special Training below. When you [**Make Camp**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp), you can retrain them to change which is marked.

- ☐ *Magical Familiar* — They count as an extension of yourself when you [**Cast a Spell**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\))
- ☐ *Sentinel* — They will always alert you to sudden dangers or ambushes before it's too late
- ☐ *Sniffer* — When you [**Unearth Secrets**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#unearth-secrets-\(+int\)), before you roll you may ask one question about the secret

#### ☐ In this Together — Loyalty ☐ ☐ ☐

When you retrain your animal companion, you can also change their Drawback.

After you **help your animal companion work on their Drawback**, gain 1 Loyalty (max 3). You may spend Loyalty to gain an unmarked Special Training until the end of the scene.

#### ☐ Kindred Spirits

When you [**Make Camp**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp), your animal companion clears one of your Conditions (your choice).

*Emotional Support* — Choose a Condition now; ignore its penalties while your animal companion is with you. You may change the chosen Condition when you retrain them.

#### ☐ Superior Companion

Your animal companion can have two Special Trainings (instead of one), and can also choose from the following:

- ☐ *Distraction* — When you [**Sneak Past**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sneak-past-\(+dex\)), after you roll you may send your companion away to draw attention, increasing the result by one level (6- → 7–9 → 10+)
- ☐ *Pack Leader* — Others of their kind treat you and your companions as allies, if not friends
- ☐ *Rescuer* — When you would [**Face Death**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death), they save you but can't help again until you [**Make Camp**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp); regain 1 HP

#### ☐ Warbeast

When your animal companion helps you to fight, increase the added armor to 2, and the added dmg to 1d6. Once per scene, when they would get hurt, they're actually fine.

### Deadeye

*One shot, one kill*

#### ☑ <a id="death-from-afar-—-aim-☐-☐-☐-☐"></a>Death from Afar — Aim ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐

When you enter a combat situation, gain Aim equal to your DEX (min 1). You can spend 1 Aim to do one of the maneuvers below. When the combat situation ends, lose all Aim.

* *Line It Up* — Your next ranged attack gains `#piercing` and ignores cover and concealment
* *Rapid Shots* — Your next ranged attack gains `#area` and either `#hand`, `#close`, `#near`, or `#far`
* *Suppressing Fire* — [**Aid a PC**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#aid-a-pc-\(n/a\)) without paying another cost; if they take dmg, reduce it by 1 (min 1)
* *Take Cover* — After you [**Trade Blows**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)) at range, reduce dmg taken by your DEX (min 0)

#### ☐ Find Weakness

You may [**Sense Motive**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sense-motive-\(+wis\))\+DEX by observing them in a battle or physical contest. If you do so, no matter the roll you may also ask one question about their strengths and weaknesses.

#### ☐ Sniper Nest

When you spend minutes or hours (GM’s call) finding or creating the perfect vantage point, you gain the following benefits while there:

* Once a combat situation begins, gain \+1 Aim (max 4)
* The first time you attack with a ranged weapon from it, no one new learns your position
* Trap kits set up here deal 1d6 dmg instead of 1d4 and never impede or harm you or your allies

When you leave the nest, lose its benefits unless you spend time setting it up again. If you need to [**Defy Danger**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#defy-danger-\(+variable\)) to leave it safely, roll with adv.

#### ☐ Spotter

When you receive [**Aid**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#aid-a-pc-\(n/a\)) while attacking with a ranged weapon, instead of gaining adv on a roll you may execute one maneuver without spending Aim.

#### ☐ Trick Arrows

You may spend Aim or mark a use of the appropriate item to do any of the following maneuvers on your next ranged attack:

* *adventuring gear* — `#forceful` or pulls you to them or vice-versa
* *alchemical vials* — \+1 dmg and spreads acid, fire, or smoke
* *poisoner’s kit* — target takes \+1 dmg until the end of the scene
* *trap kit* — \+1 dmg and briefly immobilizes your target

### Scout

*Intimately familiar with wilderness’ bounties and perils*

#### ☑ <a id="lay-of-the-land"></a>Lay of the Land

When you survey an environment or landscape away from your companions, roll+INT, then skip ahead to when you return to your companions.

* **10+:** choose three benefits below.
* **7–9:** only two.
* **6-:** choose one but something followed you back; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

- ☐ Natural wildlife won’t prey on, or flee from, you or your allies
- ☐ When you [**Sneak Past**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sneak-past-\(+dex\)) by blending in, treat a 7–9 as a 10+
- ☐ When you [**Unearth Secrets**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#unearth-secrets-\(+int\)), if the secret is related to the environment (e.g. corruption, feature, usual inhabitant, etc.), treat a 6- as a 7–9
- ☐ When you [**Undertake a Perilous Journey**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#undertake-a-perilous-journey) as the leader, gain \+1 to the roll and clear a Condition. If another PC is the leader, gain 1 Depth with them if they ask for your advice

These benefits last until you leave the environment or use this move again.

#### ☐ Hunter’s Mark — Quarry: \_\_\_

Once per session, when you focus on hunting an individual, gain the following benefits when interacting with them until the end of that session:

* Gain adv on Moves rolled in direct pursuit of them
* When you [**Spout Lore**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#spout-lore-\(+questions\)) about them, gain \+1 to the roll
* The first time this session you deal dmg to them, it's max
* When you capture or defeat them, clear a Condition

#### ☐ Peerless Wayfarer

When you use [**Lay of the Land**](#lay-of-the-land), on a **12+**, you also bring back a lead on an ally, opportunity, or treasure. Say which, then the GM will tell you more. No matter the roll, if you spend weeks, make an additional choice.

When you leave an environment you've surveyed with Lay of the Land, write down your benefits; you regain them if you ever return here.

#### ☐ The Wild Provides

When you search a natural landscape for resources, [**Defy Danger**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#defy-danger-\(+variable\))\+INT. On a **7+**, gain 2 Uses, split between adventuring gear, medicine, and provisions.

The resources you find could be foraged from wildlife, or found in a hidden cache, or another method.

#### ☐ Natural Historian

When you [**Spout Lore**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#spout-lore-\(+questions\)) about flora or fauna from the wild, on a **7+**, ask an additional question. If it can fight, the GM will also tell you its HP and dmg.
