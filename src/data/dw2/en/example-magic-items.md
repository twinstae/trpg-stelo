### <a id="example-magic-items"></a>Example Magic Items

#### adamantine armor

*This suit of medium or heavy metallic armor is reinforced with adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence* 

While you're wearing it, any time you would suffer max dmg, instead they just roll with adv.

#### alchemy jug

*A seemingly-ordinary 1 gallon ceramic jug*

When you speak its magic word, one of the following liquids fills the jug (your call):

* acid
* basic poison
* beer
* fresh water
* mayonnaise
* oil
* saltwater
* vinegar
* wine

#### animated rope

*A rope that listens and sometimes wags one of its ends like a tail. Does tricks, too, like a smart and more obedient snake might*

When you command the rope to perform a simple action (within its capabilities), it does so. Example orders include:

* coil
* come here
* slack

#### animated shield — ◯ use

*This shield has been enchanted to follow its owner around and protect them. It’s sometimes overzealous in its duty*

This shield floats near you, and protects you of its own volition without you needing to hold or wear it. Mark a use to ignore all dmg from one attack.

#### armor of vulnerability

`#cursed` *This golden suit of armor never needs to be polished* 

Choose one type of dmg from the following: divine, magical, or physical. While you're wearing it, you suffer only half the dmg of the chosen type. *Cursed*.

You cannot take this armor off unless you find a way to lift its curse. While you're wearing it, you suffer double the dmg of any type not chosen above.

#### arrow of \_\_\_ slaying

*This arrow is a magic weapon meant to slay a specific kind of creature*

Examples include: angel, demon, devil, dragon, or giant, just to name a few. Against such a creature the *arrow* has `#piercing` and, on a 12+ when you [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)), it kills the creature immediately and the *arrow*’s magic is gone.

#### arrows of acheron — ◯◯◯ uses

*Crafted in darkness by a blind fletcher, these arrows disintegrate if ever touched by the light of the sun*

Mark a use to have your arrows ignore all darkness, blindness, and concealment for a scene.

#### bag of holding

*This ordinary-looking belt pouch that smells faintly of ozone*

This bag can contain a cart’s worth of volume and weight, while still weighing and measuring as if it was empty.

#### barb of the Black Gate

*A nail or spike, twisted and forever cold, said to have been pried from the Gates of Death themselves*

When you embed the barb into a corpse, it disappears and ensures that corpse will never be risen again—no one and nothing but Death himself can reignite the flame of life (or unlife) in the body.

#### bead of force — ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ uses

*This small black sphere measures 3/4 of an inch in diameter and weighs an ounce*

Typically, 1d4+4 beads of force are found together. You can throw it up to `#near` and the *bead* explodes on impact (and is destroyed). Everyone within `#close` of the explosion is trapped inside a sphere of transparent force that fills the area until the end of the scene. The sphere can be picked up, and its magic causes it to weigh only 1 pound, regardless of the weight of creatures inside.

#### berserker axe

`#cursed` *This giant, double-headed axe has a bloodstain that always look fresh—and can’t be rubbed away*

While you wield this axe, you get \+1 to your dmg rolls and your max HP increases by your STR (min 1). *Cursed*.

You cannot wield any other weapon or let go of the axe unless you find a way to lift its curse. While you're wielding it, any time you deal dmg you or one of your allies (your call) suffers the same amount.

#### dancing sword

*A long thin blade that emits a faint humming tune, even while sheathed*

When you toss the sword into the air it begins to sing, flies around within `#near` range, and attacks your foes until the end of the scene. If it fights alone, it deals 1d6 dmg and becomes Helpless if attacked. If you fight alongside it, add 1d6 to your dmg.

#### dust of disappearance — ◯ use

*Found in a small packet, this powder resembles very fine sand*

When you throw the dust into the air, you and each creature and object within `#close` of you become invisible until the end of the scene. If someone under the effect of the *dust* [Trades Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)) or [Casts a Spell](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\)), the invisibility ends for them.

#### dust of dryness — ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ uses

*This magical sand contains a mote from the earth dimension* 

This small packet contains 1d6+4 pinches of *dust*.

When you sprinkle a pinch over a cube of water it transforms into one marble-sized pellet. The pellet's weight is negligible. You can smash the pellet against a hard surface, causing the pellet to shatter and the water to be released. When you expose a creature mostly composed of water to a pinch of *dust,* you need to [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\))\+DEX.

* **12+:** the creature’s destroyed.
* **10+:** you inflict 2d6 dmg `#piercing` on them.
* **7–9:** you inflict only 1d6 dmg `#piercing`.

#### dust of sneezing and choking — ◯ use

`#cursed` *This powder appears to be dust of disappearance*

When you throw the dust into the air, you and each creature and object within `#close` of you that needs to breathe become unable to do so while sneezing uncontrollably. You roll+DEX for everyone involved.

* **10+:** everyone escapes quickly, each suffering only 1d6 dmg `#piercing`.
* **7–9:** the escape takes longer, so everyone suffers 2d6 dmg `#piercing`.
* **6-:** everyone is at 1 HP and you all become unconscious; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### earworm wax — ◯ ◯ ◯ uses

*A yellowish candle, it seemingly never burns out and its light is strange and weak. Its wax is always cool, too*

When you drip the wax into someone’s ear, mark 1 Wax to ask your target a question and force them answer with the whole truth, despite themselves.

#### efficient quiver

*The quiver connects to an extradimensional space that allows it to hold numerous items while never weighing more than 2 pounds* 

While wearing the *efficient quiver*, any weapon with `#ammo` loses the tag.

#### elemental gem

`#consumable` *This gem contains a mote of trapped, vibrant elemental energy* 

When you break the gem, an elemental is summoned from their home dimension and the gem's magic is lost. The type of gem determines the elemental summoned: *blue sapphire* — air elemental *emerald* — water elemental *red corundum* — fire elemental *yellow diamond* — earth elemental

#### eversmoking bottle

*Smoke leaks from the lead-stoppered mouth of this brass bottle, which weighs 1 pound*

When you remove the stopper, a cloud of thick smoke pours out in a `#near` area from the bottle. The cloud's area is heavily obscured. As long as the bottle remains open and within the cloud, the area increases until it covers up to `#far`. The cloud persists as long as the bottle is open. Closing the bottle requires someone speaking a command word. Once the bottle is closed, the cloud disperses.

#### forget-me-naught — ◯ use

*This highly secretive and illegal smoky liquid is magically synthesized rather than harvested*

When you or another ingests *forget-me-naught* and focuses on a specific memory, you/they forget it forever.

#### helm of comprehending languages

*This shiny helm is inscribed with all manner of hieroglyphs, letters, and runes*

While wearing this helm you can mark a Condition to understand the literal meaning of any spoken language that you hear until the end of the scene. You also understand any written language that you see, but you must be touching the surface on which the words are written.

#### horn of blasting

*This silver horn bears the enchantment of the thunder rune*

When you speak a command word and blow the horn, mark a Condition to have it emit a thunderous blast.

Then, roll+CHA.

* **10+:** all your enemies `#near` suffer 1d6 dmg `#piercing` and are deafened until the end of the scene, and all your allies clear a Condition.
* **7–9:** choose one or the other.
* **6-:** you get both but the horn explodes, causing you to suffer the same dmg as your enemies and losing all its magic permanently; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### immovable rod — ◯ use

*A funny metal rod with a small button on it*

When you press the rod’s button, it freezes in place—in midair, standing up or lying down. It can’t be moved. Pull it, push it, try as hard as you like, the rod stays. Maybe it can be destroyed, maybe it can’t. Push the button again and it’s free to move once more.

#### magic wand — ◯ use

*Wizards and politicians have spent generations debating the risks and benefits of anyone being able to use magic with these*

A wand acts like a *spell scroll (minor)* but isn’t consumed when used. When you first receive the wand, you learn which spell it can cast. When you unleash the magic within the wand, choose one: The wand drains itself, and can’t be used again until you next successfully [Make Camp](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp) The wand drains you; mark a Condition

#### marvelous pigments — ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ uses

*Typically found in 1d4 pots inside a fine wooden box with a brush (weighing 1 pound in total)*

These pigments allow you to create three-dimensional objects by painting them in two dimensions. When you complete the painting, mark 1 use and the object or terrain feature depicted becomes a permanent, nonmagical part of reality.

#### mithral armor

*This suit of medium or heavy metallic armor is made of mithral, a light and flexible metal*

A mithral chain shirt or breastplate can be worn under normal clothes. Any mithral armor has no STR requirement to be used properly.

#### oil of slipperiness — ◯ use

*This sticky black unguent is thick and heavy in the container, but it flows quickly when poured*

Mark the use to cover someone of human size or smaller (along with the equipment they’re wearing and carrying). Until they [Make Camp](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp), the target cannot be slowed down, paralyzed, or restrained. Alternatively, the oil can be poured on the ground, covering up to `#close`. Anyone inside this area—or who enters it—falls prone. This effect lasts until the end of the scene.

#### philter of love — ◯ use

*This philter's rose-hued, effervescent liquid contains one easy-to-miss bubble shaped like a heart*

When someone drinks this philter, the next time the imbiber sees someone they become charmed until the end of the scene. If the imbiber’s alloromantic, they regard that person as their true love while this effect lasts.

#### <a id="phylactery"></a>phylactery

*You've bound your soul to a `#hidden` `#warded` vessel and escaped the grasp of true death.*

*Special: This magic item can only be gained through* Shed This Mortal Coil *from the Wizard's Necromancer Path.* While your phylactery is intact, gain the following: After you die, you return from death in a newly reformed body, and rejoin your allies the next time they [Make Camp](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp). You still lock a Condition from [Facing Death](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death). When you [Cast a Spell](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\)) using both a substance and a verb from the Necromancy School, treat the mag as if it were 1 lower when comparing it to your Casting Stat If your Phylactery is ever destroyed, you can spend (8 \- Necromancer Moves gained) Treasure to create a new one when you [Enjoy Downtime](/ko/awe/dw2/campaign-moves#enjoy-downtime).

#### portable hole

*A silky black cloth, folded like a handkerchief, that can unfold to 6 feet across*

When you spread the full cloth across a surface, such as a wall or floor, it becomes a 10ft deep extradimensional pit. Fold it up again, and anyone or anything inside is trapped if they don’t escape in time.

#### potion of animal friendship — ◯ use

*Agitating this muddy liquid brings little bits into view: a fish scale, a hummingbird tongue, a cat claw, or a squirrel hair*

When you drink this potion, you can charm one beast you can see until the end of the scene. While charmed, the beast will try to fulfill your commands to the best of their ability.

#### potion of clairvoyance — ◯ use

*An eyeball bobs in this yellowish liquid, but vanishes when the potion is opened*

When you drink this potion, you choose seeing or hearing. Until the end of the scene, you can either see or hear in a location you’re familiar with or in an obvious location unfamiliar to you until the end of the scene.

#### potion of climbing — ◯ use

*The potion is separated into brown, silver, and gray layers resembling bands of stone*

When you drink this potion, you can climb any surface at the same speed you usually walk until the end of the scene.

#### potion of diminution — ◯ use

*The red in the potion's liquid continuously contracts to a tiny bead and then expands to color the clear liquid around it*

When you drink this potion, your size is halved in all dimensions, and your weight is reduced to one-eighth of normal until you next Make Camp. You get adv to Sneak Past while this effect lasts. Everything you’re wearing and carrying changes size with you, and returns to normal when you drop it.

#### potion of flying — ◯ use

*This potion's clear liquid floats at the top of its container and has cloudy white impurities drifting in it*

When you drink this potion, you can fly and hover at the same speed you usually walk until the end of the scene.

#### potion of gaseous form — ◯ use

*This potion's container seems to hold fog that moves and pours like water*

When you drink this potion, you and everything you’re wearing and carrying transforms into an `#incorporeal` misty cloud until the end of the scene.

#### potion of growth — ◯ use

*The red in the potion's liquid continuously contracts to a tiny bead and then expands to color the clear liquid around it*

When you drink this potion, your size doubles in all dimensions, and your weight is multiplied by 8 until you next Make Camp. You inflict \+1d4 dmg while this effect lasts. Everything you’re wearing and carrying changes size with you, and returns to normal when you drop it.

#### potion of healing — ◯ use

*The potion's red liquid glimmers when agitated*

When you drink this potion, recover half your lost HP (rounded down) or clear one locked Condition.

#### potion of heroism — ◯ use

*This blue potion bubbles and steams as if boiling*

When you drink this potion, you get \+1d4 to your next Move roll and to your next dmg roll. Also, the next time you Face Death, if you choose to *Gamble with Death*, you get adv.

#### potion of invisibility — ◯ use

*This potion's container looks empty but feels as though it holds liquid*

When you drink this potion, you become invisible until you Cast a Spell, inflict dmg, Trade Blows, or the scene ends. Anything you wear or carry is invisible with you.

#### potion of poison — ◯ use

`#cursed` *This concoction looks, smells, and tastes like a potion of healing or other beneficial potion*

When you drink this potion, roll+STR.

* **10+:** you were able to withstand most of the poison’s effect; suffer only 1d6 dmg `#piercing`.
* **7–9:** you suffer 2d6 dmg `#piercing` and are `#poisoned` (dis to all rolls until you stop being poisoned).
* **6-:** you need to Face Death; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### potion of \_\_\_ resistance — ◯ use

*The color of this dense liquid varies depending on the type*

When you drink this potion, any dmg rolls of the chosen type get dis against you until the end of the scene. The types (and potion colors) include: acid (caustic green) fire (molten red) holy (golden yellow) lighting (electric blue) magic (jade black) poison (mud green) psychic (wild rose) unholy (crimson red)

#### potion of speed — ◯ use

*The potion's yellow fluid is streaked with black and swirls on its own*

When you drink this potion, you can move twice as quickly, you get \+1 to your armor and adv to any DEX-based rolls until the scene ends. When the effect ends, mark Exhausted.

#### potion of water breathing — ◯ use

*Its cloudy green fluid smells of the sea and has a jellyfish-like bubble floating in it*

When you drink this potion, you can breathe underwater until the end of the scene.

#### restorative ointment — ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ uses

*This glass jar, 3 inches in diameter, contains 1d4+1 doses of a thick mixture that smells faintly of aloe. The jar and its contents weigh 1/2 pound*

When you apply to the skin or swallow a dose, you recover 1d8 HP, stop being `#poisoned`, and are cure of any disease.

#### ring of warmth

*Three miniature white arctic foxes curled together on the outer side of a ring lined with fluffy white fur on the inside*

While wearing this ring you don’t mark Conditions or suffer dmg from extremely cold environments. In addition, everything you wear and carry are unharmed by low temperatures.

#### sending stones

*Two identical marbles with matching symbols carved into them*

When you speak into one stone, the other emits your voice.

#### sovereign glue — ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ uses

*This viscous, milky-white substance can form a permanent adhesive bond between any two objects*

When found, a container has 1d6+1 uses left. One use can cover a 1-foot square surface and it takes 1 minute to set. Once it has done so, the bond it creates can only be broken by the application of *universal solvent* or *oil of etherealness*.

#### spell scroll (minor) — ◯ use

*An ancient-looking parchment scroll sealed with a pale-white ribbon and a golden wax seal impressed with an unknown symbol*

When you read this scroll, its magic is consumed and you can [Cast a Spell](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\))\+INT with an effect of mag 0 or 1\. The spell scroll is considered a magic item you can use and on a 7-9, it doesn’t grant you a unique option.

#### staff of the adder

*A wood staff carved into the face of a snake*

When you wield and command this staff, it transforms into a venomous snake which understands and follows your commands until the end of the scene. Venomous Snake — 3 HP, 1 Armor (bark-like skin), 1d4 dmg (bite). *Poison*.

PCs bitten must roll+STR.

* **7–9:** they suffer 1d4 extra poison dmg.
* **6-:** they suffer 2d4 extra poison dmg

#### sword of life stealing

*This blade’s crossguard and pommel are ornate, decorated with skull motifs. The grip is wrapped in black material with red accents and tied with red ribbons*

While you wield this sword when you Trade Blows, on a 12+, you inflict \+1d6 dmg as long as your opponent isn’t a construct or undead, and you regain HP equal to the total dmg you inflicted.

#### tears of Annalise

*Cloudy red gemstones the size of a thumbnail, the tears of Annalise are always found in pairs*

When you and another PC each swallow a tear, you can feel the other’s strong emotions, particularly sadness, loss, fear, or desire. Whenever one of you marks a Condition, the other may mark one instead. The effects last until one spills the blood of the other.

#### trident of fish command — ◯ ◯ ◯ uses

*This trident has a staff made of dark, weathered wood, adorned with rough, organic textures and sharp, dark green barnacle-like growths near the base*

While you wield this trident, you can mark a use and roll+WIS to telepathically connect with a beast native to water.

* **10+:** you take total control of the creature’s actions until the end of the scene.
* **7–9:** you can give the creature a single, simple command that they’ll follow to the best of their ability.
* **6-:** the creature sees you as a menace; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### universal solvent — ◯ use

*This tube holds milky liquid with a strong alcohol smell*

When you mark a use and pour the contents of the tube onto a surface within reach, the liquid instantly dissolves up to 1 square foot of adhesive it touches, including *sovereign glue*.

#### vicious weapon

*A weapon with an ornate gold basket hilt and a long, thin steel blade*

While you wield this weapon when you [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)), on a 12+, you inflict \+1d6 dmg.

### <a id="example-artifacts"></a>Example Artifacts

#### Alveric’s Soulstealer Blade — Trapped Souls ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐

*This obsidian, rune-engraved weapon is rumored to be more than a mere instrument of destruction*

**Devourer of Souls.** When you take a person's life with Soulstealer, it absorbs their soul; mark a box in the Trapped Souls track above. When you mark the fifth box, clear the track and unlock one of the Moves below.

While wielding it, you can replace your STR stat with the number of Soulstealer Moves you have unlocked (min. \+1, max. \+4).

- ☐ **Gravity-Defying Menace.** Clear one Trapped Soul to have Soulstealer fight on its own or fly to your hand from afar.
- ☐ **Reality Rifter.** When you use Soulstealer to open a rift in reality, connecting the world you’re currently in with another you know of, treat it as [Perform a Ritual](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#perform-a-ritual) with the blade as the font of magical power. After the GM tells you the requirements you may clear Trapped Souls, 1-for-1, to profanely fulfill them.
- ☐ **The Great Banisher.** When you [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)) while wielding Soulstealer, and your opponent is a Bloodied outsider, on a 10+, you can banish them from the Material world permanently. Mark a box in the Trapped Souls track. They cannot manifest again until you die or that box is cleared.

#### Caliburnus, The Sword That Heals — Ruin ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐

*A legendary sword that can only wielded by those the weapon deems worthy*

**Rightful Touch.** When you touch someone you deem worthy with Caliburnus’ flat side, mark Ruin so they recover Xd6 HP, where X equals your CHA. When you mark the fifth box, clear the track and unlock one of the Moves below. Caliburnus is virtually indestructible.

While wielding it, you can replace your CHA stat with the number of Caliburnus Moves you have available (min \+1, max \+4).

- ☐ **A New Dawn.** When you raise Caliburnus towards the sky and cry out for light, the blade lights up with the radiance of thirty torches; mark Ruin and roll+CHA.
    * **10+:** choose two from below.
    * **7–9:** only one.
    * **6-:** the light draws the ire of evil forces; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.
    - The shining light illuminates a secret, treasure, or opportunity
    - You and all your allies up to `#far` clear a Condition
    - Each evildoer up to `#far` suffers 1d6 `#holy` `#piercing` dmg
- ☐ **Bright Eyes.** You may mark Ruin to see through any concealment, deception, or illusion, as well as feel the presence of magic all around you.
- ☐ **Promised Victory.** When you would [Face Death](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death), you can mark Ruin equal to your CHA and remain at 1 HP instead.

If you ever reach 5 Ruin and have no further Moves to take, your ruin has finally come. Your character must leave the Material world and Caliburnus must be returned to its keeper.

#### Elleovene’s Circlet of Supreme Mesmerism — Stone Heart ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐

*An apparently simple silver circlet with a blue jewel at its center*

**Awakened Mind.** You can read sapient beings’ superficial thoughts and you can focus on someone `#near` to delve into their deeper mind. For the purposes of [Sensing Motive](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sense-motive-\(+wis\)) of someone `#near`, this focus counts as intimate or prying conversation.

You no longer mark Conditions. When instructed to do so, you can either mark Stone Heart above or lock a Condition. When you mark the fifth Stone Heart box, clear the track and unlock one of the Moves below.

- ☐ **Master of Puppets.** When you touch someone skin-to-skin and force your mind on them, roll+WIS. On a 7+, choose one below. On a 7–9, mark Stone Heart due to the effort. On a 6-, mark 1 XP and they choose one for you instead.
    * They answer two of your questions as if you [Sensed their Motive](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sense-motive-\(+wis\))
    * They follow a simple command until the scene ends
    * They forget a memory
- ☐ **Spy the World’s Mind Web.** When you expand your mind to sense all of the Material world’s mind, roll+WIS. On a 7+, the GM will tell you one detail of a Threat (Asset, Development, or Reaction). On a 7–9, you also choose one below. On a 6-, something or someone’s blocking you; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.
    * Somebody unwelcome knows of your spying
    * You suffer from psychic whiplash; mark 2 Stone Heart boxes
- ☐ **Willbreaker.** When you unleash your mind's power upon someone `#near`, roll+WIS. On a 10+, choose two from below. On a 7–9, only one. On a 6-, they were somehow ready for you; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.
    * They can’t act until they’re harmed or the scene ends
    * They suffer 1d10 `#piercing` dmg from mental overload
    * You can use this again this scene (otherwise you can’t)

If you would ever mark the fifth box and you have no further Moves to take, you lose your character, as you become an NPC that’s transcended the mortal condition.

#### Indelible Mark of Drakon — Spreading Scales ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐

*A gleaming, draconic scale made of an unknown, indestructible metal, forever searching for a new worthy host*

**Draconic Desires.** After you get the Mark embedded in your body (willingly or unwillingly), you feel its influence. You mark Spreading Scales when any of the following is true:

* You [End the Session](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#end-the-session) without having spent or gained Depth
* You [Reach Your Limit](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#reach-your-limit)
* You refuse [Comfort or Support](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#comfort-or-support)

When you mark the fifth box, clear the track and unlock one of the Moves below.

You get extra HP equal to the number of Mark Moves you have available (min. \+1, max. \+4). You can clear a marked Spreading Scales to reduce dmg you suffer to min (even if it was max).

- ☐ **Claws Like Steel.** Reptilian, razor sharp talons replace your usual hands and feet. When you [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)) with them, you can clear a marked Spreading Scale to inflict 1d6 dmg `#piercing` (and roll dmg with adv if you attack with both).
- ☐ **Fire Tongue.** You now know the ancient tongue of dragons. You can clear a marked Spreading Scale to spew flames, inflicting 1d12 dmg `#piercing` `#close`.
- ☐ **Sinewy Wings.** Additional draconic appendages materialize on your back. You can clear a marked Spreading Scale to fly freely until the end of the scene.

If you would ever mark the fifth box and you have no further Moves to take, you lose your character. They become an NPC dragon under the GM’s control.

#### Gwendolen’s Branch of the Green — Growth ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐

*An everchanging, seemingly innocuous tree branch which contains the unbound power of Nature*

**Spring’s Saplings.** While holding this youthful wand, you can mark Growth to [Shapeshift](/ko/awe/dw2/the-druid#shapeshift) (as per the Druid Starting Move) as if you rolled a 10+. If you already have Shapeshift, you can now transform into a hybrid form between mortal and beast, that grants you both the benefits of the chosen adaptation and all your normal capacities.

When you mark the fifth Growth box, clear the track and unlock the next Move in order from top to bottom. Clear the previous Move this branch had; the cycle is eternal.

- ☐ **Summer’s Blooms.** While holding this now robust staff, you can mark Growth to intervene and miraculously save someone who shouldn’t die yet who’s [Facing Death](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death) `#close` to you. They regain 1 HP.
- ☐ **Autumn’s Fruits.** While holding this now diminished rod, you can mark Growth to [Cast a Spell](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\)) by entreating the natural spirits for aid (as per the [Anima Mundi](/ko/awe/dw2/the-druid#anima-mundi) in the Animist Druid Path). Once per session, you can also ignore the questions and treat the result as if you rolled a 10+ with mag up to your WIS.
- ☐ **Winter’s Solace.** While holding this now withered wand, you can mark Growth to end an unnatural creature’s life who’s Bloodied. When you mark the fifth Growth box, the Branch crumbles to ashes until only a seedling remains. You need to plant the seed so someone else can have access to a new Branch in the future.

#### Lazurite Dragon Orb — Mastery ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐

*An etched crystal globe with blue mist whirling inside*

Once per session, when you confront the blue dragon trapped in the Orb, roll+INT.

* **10+:** mark 3 Mastery over them.
* **7–9:** only 2.
* **6-:** mark 1, but the dragon clouds your mind; lock Angry.

When you mark the fifth box, clear the track and unlock one of the Moves below. As long as you have any Mastery marked, you’re considered to be In Control.

- ☐ **Draconic Might.** As long as you’re In Control, you can clear Mastery to improve a [Defy Danger](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#defy-danger-\(+variable\)) or [Trade Blows](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)) by a number of steps equal to the number of boxes cleared (min 1, max 2). You can also clear Mastery to ignore all dmg from a draconic source.
- ☐ **Draconic Magic.** As long as you’re In Control, you can [Cast a Spell](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\))\+INT by changing reality through words of draconic power; clear Mastery equal to the spell’s mag (min 1, max 4).
- ☐ **Draconic Power.** As long as you’re In Control, you can unleash a jumping lightning bolt that inflicts Xd6 `#piercing` dmg to X creatures up to `#far`, where X is the amount of Mastery boxes cleared. You can also clear a Mastery box to ignore all dmg from a dragon’s breath.
- ☐ **Draconic Sway.** As long as you’re In Control, you can give an undeniable order to a dragon up to `#far`. To do so, clear a number of Mastery boxes equal to a dragon’s power (min 1, max 4); then it must follow the order. You can also clear a Mastery box to know whether there are any dragons up to 10 miles away.
