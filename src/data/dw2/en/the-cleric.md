### Starting Move

#### ☑ <a id="invoke-the-divine-—-name-(pro/nouns):-___"></a>Invoke the Divine — Name (pro/nouns): \_\_\_

You have a close connection with a deity who empowers you to spread their ideals. Give them a name and pronouns, then choose one per row below (or write your own): Domain: ☐ Craft ☐ Law ☐ Light ☐ Nature ☐ Travel Worshippers: ☐ artists ☐ explorers ☐ farmers ☐ merchants ☐ nobles Enemies: ☐ bandits ☐ industrialists ☐ nocturnal monsters ☐ rebels ☐ undead You can Cast a Spell\+WIS with any effect under your deity’s Domain(s). To Cast a Spell you need to invoke your deity’s name aloud. Write a 2-Depth Relationship with them. When you Cast a Spell this way, your unique 7-9 option is: *your deity disapproves*; erase 1 Depth with them You can gain Depth with your deity by protecting their worshippers, spreading their faith, and vanquishing their enemies (GM’s call).

"*Any effect under your deity’s Domain(s)*" is intentionally broad. Here are some examples for each domain:

* Law —  *command someone, detect deceit, summon enforcers*  
* Light — *light up a room, conjure light-based illusions, call down searing light from above*  
* Craft — *assemble or create objects, dismantle crafted objects, know who created something*  
* Nature — *rapidly grow vegetation, summon animals to your aid, change the weather*  
* Travel — *find or create shelter, carve a path through a hazard or obstacle, teleport somewhere*

### Caregiver

*What good is a faith that doesn’t make the world whole?*

#### ☑ The Greatest Miracle — Name (pro/nouns): \_\_\_

Gain the [Invoke the Divine](#invoke-the-divine-—-name-\(pro/nouns\):-___) Cleric Starting Move if you don't already have it, except your deity's domain is Life. Choose your deity's worshippers and enemies as detailed in the Move. If you already have [Invoke the Divine](#invoke-the-divine-—-name-\(pro/nouns\):-___), add Life to your deity's domains. *Is this a new aspect of your deity, a result of your unique connection to them, or something else?* *Battlefield Medic* — Once per session, when you endanger yourself to protect or heal someone, gain 1 Depth with your deity. *Cure Wounds* — Whenever you [Cast a Spell](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\)) to heal someone (e.g. restore HP, cure disease, etc.), treat the mag as if it were 1 lower when comparing it to your Casting Stat.

#### ☐ Awake\! The Shadow Is Gone

When someone else [Faces Death](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death) in your presence, you may parley with Death and grant the other PC access to the following option: *Revivified* — You’re no longer Helpless and you recover half your max HP. You still lock a Condition after [Facing Death](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death) If they choose this option, you cannot use this Move again until you’ve settled your deal with Death.

#### ☐ Face of an Angel

No one will attack you directly and unprovoked, unless they’ve seen you fight or have a score to settle. When you [Pull Strings](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#pull-strings-\(+cha\)), you get access to the following approach: If you *implore them to turn away from violence*, they might leave now or change their mind later.

#### ☐ I'll Carry it For You

When you touch someone else and offer yourself as a sacrifice, erase 1 Depth with your deity then do either or both: Choose any Conditions they have; they clear those Conditions and you mark them Choose any amount of HP they are missing; they restore it, and you lose them

#### ☐ Mass Cure Wounds

When you proclaim your faith, erase 1 Depth with your deity to restore half of your and your `#close` allies maximum HP (rounded down).

### Oracle

*Things that were, are, and yet may be*

#### ☑ Visions From Above — Name (pro/nouns): \_\_\_

Gain the [Invoke the Divine](#invoke-the-divine-—-name-\(pro/nouns\):-___) Cleric Starting Move if you don't already have it, except your deity's domain is Knowledge. Choose your deity's worshippers and enemies as detailed in the Move. If you already have [Invoke the Divine](#invoke-the-divine-—-name-\(pro/nouns\):-___), add Knowledge to your deity's domains. *Is this a new aspect of your deity, a result of your unique connection to them, or something else? Guidance* — Now and at the start of each session, roll 1d6 as your Fate Die and keep its result. Once per scene, after someone rolls for a Move (not for dmg or similar), you may switch one die with your Fate Die. The die that was switched out becomes your new Fate Die.

#### ☐ A Moth to the Sacred Flame

When you willingly expose yourself to an enemy attack, roll+CHA; if you’re vulnerable while doing so, roll with adv. \
* **10+:** they boast and babble at you, and you can ask any two questions about them or their superiors. \
* **7–9:** you can ask one question, but after answering they’ll turn to violence. \
* **6-:** they’re far more cunning or powerful than they appear (GM’s call); mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ Haruspication

When you ritually sacrifice something important, ask your deity one question about your sacrifice or something associated with it (owners, loved ones, history, etc.), and roll+WIS. \
* **10+:** your deity shows you a vivid vision that answers your question fully. \
* **7–9:** the vision is cryptic and needs deciphering. \
* **6-:** your vision includes an omen of doom; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ Sacred Truth

Once per session, when you publicly reveal the truth about a secret or mystery, gain 1 Depth with your deity. If someone present doesn't want it known, you also gain adv on your next rolled Move against them.

#### ☐ True Colors

When you [Sense Motive](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#sense-motive-\(+wis\)), you can also ask one of the following questions, even on a 6-. *What are your true feelings about me? What do you not want to admit* (to me, to another, to yourself, etc.)? *Who or what do you care for most of all?*

### Warpriest

*Evil cannot be tolerated*

#### ☑ Punishment Divine — Name (pro/nouns): \_\_\_

Gain the [Invoke the Divine](#invoke-the-divine-—-name-\(pro/nouns\):-___) Cleric Starting Move if you don't already have it, except your deity's domain is War. Choose your deity's worshippers and enemies as detailed in the Move. If you already have [Invoke the Divine](#invoke-the-divine-—-name-\(pro/nouns\):-___), add War to your deity's domains. *Is this a new aspect of your deity, a result of your unique connection to them, or something else? Divine Shield* — Once per scene, when you take dmg, you may erase 1 Depth with your deity to ignore it, even if it is `#piercing`. *Rebuke* — Once per session, when you condemn someone for their evil ways, gain 1 Depth with your deity.

#### ☐ Divine Intuition

When you first encounter an enemy of your deity, you may ask the GM questions about it equal to your WIS. You get adv on your first roll related to the answer(s).

#### ☐ Drink From the Cup of Wrath

When you condemn someone for their evil ways, instead of gaining 1 Depth with your deity, you may make the condemned an enemy of your deity (if they aren't already). When you petition your deity to harm or curse a collective enemy (e.g. a faction, creature type, inhabitants of a location, etc.), treat it as [Perform a Ritual](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#perform-a-ritual), but after the GM tells you the requirements you may erase Depth with your deity, 1-for-1, to miraculously fulfill them.

#### ☐ Not the End

When you [Face Death](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death), you can also choose the following option: *Divine Intervention*. Your deity miraculously saves your life, banishes a danger, enemy or obstacle, then restores all your HP. You’re no longer Helpless. You still lock a Condition after [Facing Death](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death).

#### ☐ Spiritual Weapon

When you shout your deity’s name like a battle cry, their power fills any weapon you wield until the end of the scene. Choose one blessing below, or erase 1 Depth with your deity to choose two. Add \+1 to all dmg it deals It `#pierces` the armor of your deity's enemies It can hover and fight on its own as an extension of your will; treat it as a loyal NPC, when it takes dmg it loses this benefit unless you feed it your lifeforce to continue (lose 1 HP)

Like with all NPCs, if you choose the third option your spiritual weapon doesn't roll a Move to fight another NPC. The GM will say what will happen in the fiction and deal damage as appropriate.
