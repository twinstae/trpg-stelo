## <a id="campaign-moves"></a>Campaign Moves

### <a id="enjoy-downtime"></a>Enjoy Downtime

When you spend a few days resting in a safe place, you may do as many as you’d like of the following: *Carouse*. Spend 1 Treasure to clear a locked Condition or gain 1 Depth with another PC. *Resupply*. Spend 1 Treasure to refresh all uses on a PC’s non-magic items. *Shop*. Spend 1 Treasure to repair equipment, purchase items, or enlist services; you spend 1 Treasure per good/service. *Train*. Once per Downtime each PC can spend 1 Treasure to mark XP.

In order to count as safe enough to trigger this Move, you usually need to rest in a well-guarded place without any imminent Threats. A makeshift camp in the wilderness isn't safe enough to really relax, no matter how hidden or well-defended you make it.

### <a id="forge-a-bond"></a>Forge a Bond

When two PCs have 3 Depth with the each other, clear both tracks and the Relationships, then choose one: *Establish a new Bond*. Choose a Bond; write it on both character sheets in place of the Relationships. If you shared another Bond, erase it; both of you mark 1 XP for each Bond Advanced Move it had unlocked. *Reinforce an existing Bond*. Unlock a new Bond Advanced Move from it. *“How are you* really *feeling?”* Ask each other at least one question about your character’s thoughts, feelings, or motivations. If both answer truthfully, each marks 1 XP or clears 1 locked Condition.

If one PC already has 3 Depth with another, they won't trigger this Move until that 3 Depth is matched in kind. If they would gain a 4th Depth with that PC, nothing happens. Remember that you can spend Depth to [**Aid a PC**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#aid-a-pc-\(n/a\)).

[**Example Bonds**](/ko/awe/dw2/bonds#example-bonds) can be found below.

### <a id="reveal-your-struggle"></a>Reveal Your Struggle

When you [Make Camp](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp) with a locked Condition, or when you [Level Up](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#level-up), you may choose to confess a new, terrible truth about your PC’s Struggle, If you do, reveal a new Struggle or add a new detail to an already revealed one (and clear the locked Condition if any). You can only have one revealed Struggle at a time. When your revealed Struggle is resolved, [Level Up](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#level-up) (keeping marked XP), erase the Struggle, and lose both its Benefit (+) and Drawback (-). Clear the Advancement box if marked; you can choose a new Struggle in the future.

**Example Struggles** can be found below. 

It's up to you what terrible truth you want to confess about your Struggle, but here are some examples:

* A powerful enemy knows all about your Struggle and how to exploit it  
* An important NPC in the world shares or reflects the worst aspects of your Struggle  
* Something about your Struggle was a lie; the truth is much worse  
* There's another aspect of your Struggle that makes resolving it even more complicated  
* You once did something terrible because of your Struggle  
* You find evidence that your Struggle will only end in doom  
* You're Struggle has put you, and maybe your allies, in much deeper trouble than anyone thought
