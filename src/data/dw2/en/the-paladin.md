### Starting Move

#### ☑ <a id="oaths-of-conviction-—-liege:-___"></a>Oaths of Conviction — Liege: \_\_\_

You have sworn righteous oaths and service to a liege; name them now. Example lieges include: *a visionary leader; an august institution; a subversive organization, etc.*

You start with all five of the following oaths marked. As long as you uphold an oath, you can use its benefit:

- ☑ **Bravery** (*Always be the first one in and the last one out*). When you [**Defy Danger**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#defy-danger-\(+variable\)), treat any 6- as a 7–9
- ☑ **Mercy** (*Never take a life*). ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ ◯ uses. Max uses equal to twice your WIS (min 1). Mark one to touch someone with a bare hand and restore 1 HP. Regain all uses when you finish [**Making Camp**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp)
- ☑ **Protection** (*Never let an innocent die in your presence*). Your weapons deal `#holy` dmg and add \+1 to your armor
- ☑ **Purity** (*Never trick or deceive*). You're immune to all diseases and you can mark a Condition to cure someone else’s disease
- ☑ **Retribution** (*Never let an evildoer go unpunished*). You can mark a Condition to perceive creatures with evil intent up to `#near` you. When you declare someone to have evil intent, from now on they suffer 1d4 dmg `#piercing` whenever they inflict dmg on you or your `#close` allies

When you break an oath (your call), clear that oath and lock a Condition. When you find a way to atone (also your call), clear a Condition and mark a cleared oath.

### Inquisitor

*Rotten apples must be purged before they spoil the rest*

#### ☑ Beware My Power

If you don't have it, gain the [**Oaths of Conviction**](#oaths-of-conviction-—-liege:-___) Paladin Starting Move, but with only the oath below. If you already have that Move, add this oath as well:

- ☑ **Castigation** (*Never allow an evildoer to live*). When you [**Trade Blows**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)) with a creature with evil intent (GM's call), add \+1d6 `#holy` dmg

*Smite Evil* — When you [**Trade Blows**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#trade-blows-\(+str-or-+dex\)) with a creature with evil intent (GM's call), on a **10+**, add the following to the list of options available:

* If they’re an outsider to this world, you banish them for a while
* You mark their appearance permanently; describe how
* You weaken their resolve; the GM will say what happens

#### ☐ By the Power Invested in Me…

When you give an NPC an order based on your authority, roll+STR.

* **10+:** they follow your order and you get adv to your next Move rolled against them.
* **7–9:** they choose one:
* **6-:** they dismiss your authority; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

- Back away cautiously, then flee
- Consult with another authority
- Do as you say, but tell someone about it afterwards

#### ☐ Mystical Imprisonment

When you name an evildoer and order them to stop, magical chains appear and bind them; roll+STR.

* **10+:** choose two below.
* **7–9:** only one.
* **6-:** the chains can't hold them; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

- It lasts longer than a few moments
- They can't move
- They can't speak or cast spells

#### ☐ Nomosmancy

You can [**Cast a Spell**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#cast-a-spell-\(+variable\))\+STR to impose Order and deny a supernatural effect. When you do so, your unique 7–9 option is:

* You get a supernatural stain; you cannot use this Move until you [**Make Camp**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp)

#### ☐ Public Condemnation

When you proclaim someone to be an evildoer to the public, roll+STR.

* **10+:** choose two from the list below.
* **7–9:** only one.
* **6-:** an evildoer is hidden among the crowd; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

- They will hunt the evildoer down for you as a group
- They will protect you from the evildoer
- You impress someone powerful (GM's call); roll with adv the next time you [**Pull Strings**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#pull-strings-\(+cha\)) on them

### Paragon

*An inspiration to anyone who meets you*

#### ☑ Safeguard the Helpless

If you don't have it, gain the [**Oaths of Conviction**](#oaths-of-conviction-—-liege:-___) Paladin Starting Move, but with only the Protection oath. If you already have that oath, its benefits also stop foes from `#piercing` your armor.

*Hold the Line* — When a `#near` NPC attempts to harm or move towards an ally, you may interpose and roll+CHA.

* **10+:** they can't get past you.
* **7–9:** they get past you, but their next dmg dealt is halved (rounded down).
* **6-:** they find an opening against you and your ally; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ A Shot at Redemption

When you offer a defeated foe an opportunity to change their ways, offer them proof of your sincerity and roll+CHA.

* **10+:** they’ll make their best attempt at it.
* **7–9:** they’re interested but will need constant monitoring and guidance.
* **6-:** they aren’t ready; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ “For the Honor of…”

Once per session, when you assume a heroic form related to your Origins, choose benefits from below up to your CHA, which last until you [**Face Death**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death), [**Reach Your Limit**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#reach-your-limit), or the scene ends, whichever happens first.

* All allies who witness it clear a Condition
* All foes who witness it ignore your allies for now and treat you as the biggest threat
* Reduce dmg taken from NPCs with evil intent by half (rounded down)
* Your STR is \+3

#### ☐ Inspiring Presence

When you rally your allies with a show of commitment, roll+CHA.

* **10+:** all of them clear a Condition.
* **7–9:** you can mark a Condition so up to your CHA of them clear a Condition.
* **6-:** your action is ignored; mark 1 XP and the GM makes a Move.

#### ☐ You Will Not Break Me

When you [**Reach Your Limit**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#reach-your-limit), you may also choose from the options below. If you do, you only clear up to CHA Conditions, but may mark 1 Depth with a PC who helped you:

* **Angry** — Make a show of force or lash out at your foes
* **Distracted** — Pursue a suspicion or side-quest
* **Exhausted** — Sound the retreat or make a personal sacrifice
* **Frightened** — Confront your fears or confide in another
* **Insecure** — Seek guidance or submit to authority

### Questing Knight

*Absolute commitment can achieve the impossible*

#### ☑ On a Mission: \_\_\_

When you dedicate yourself to a quest for your liege, describe it. If you already have a quest, erase it and mark a Condition. Then mark blessings up to your WIS. They remain while you stay true to your quest, and can be changed when you [**Make Camp**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#make-camp).

- ☐ *Ardent Dedication* — You know the direction of your quest
- ☐ *Immaculate Body* — You are immune to a specific type of harm (fire, falling, arrows, etc.)
- ☐ *Golden Tongue* — You can communicate with any sapient being
- ☐ *Seventh Sense* — You can sense the presence of a creature type
- ☐ *Superhuman State* — You don’t need food, water, or sleep

When you [**End the Session**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#end-the-session), mark 1 XP if you’ve made significant progress on your quest. When you finish your quest, mark 2 XP, recover all HP, clear a locked Condition, and lose your blessings.

#### ☐ I Dub Thee…

When you [**Face Death**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#face-death), you may also choose the following:

* *Newly Knighted* — Name a PC, say why you believe in them, then die. If they accept, they gain this Path (beyond normal Path limits).

#### ☐ The Great Uniter

When you try to join or ally with a faction, or request assistance from your liege, if they aren’t your direct foes they’ll tell you of a big problem or goal they’re grappling with. If you help them resolve it, you earn their loyalty, and they’re willing to work alongside anyone, even their enemies, as long as you lead the efforts.

#### ☐ Throw Down the Gauntlet

When you demand a one-on-one duel in response to an insult, your opponent cannot decline, but they can use a substitute, propose provisions, or add complications (GM’s call). Additionally, whenever you begin the duel, clear Conditions up to your WIS.

#### ☐ Trusted Squire — Name (pro/nouns): \_\_\_ XP — ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐

You have a young NPC at your service who carries your belongings, tends to your needs, and is learning to fight (1d4 dmg). When they suffer harm, mark a Condition.

Once per session, when you teach them a lesson, [**Defy Danger**](/ko/awe/dw2/core-moves#defy-danger-\(+variable\))\+WIS. On a **7+**, they mark 1 XP. When the squire marks their 5th XP, they’re ready. You [**Level Up**](/ko/awe/dw2/extra-moves#level-up) when you sponsor them and they leave to become a paladin themselves. Lose access to this Move until you take on a new squire.
